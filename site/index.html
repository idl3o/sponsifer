Sponsifer
For streamers Check a report

A sponsor was on your stream. Now prove it.

Sponsifer puts a sponsor's placement on stream through OBS, writes down every second it was on air, and signs that record with the streamer's own SSH key. The sponsor checks it here, or with ssh-keygen, which is already on their computer.

I stream Install it, put a sponsor into OBS, log the stream, and send a report the sponsor can check. I was sent a report Check a delivery report or licence receipt in this browser. Nothing you choose leaves it.

What a report is

An index into the recording. For each placement it gives the UTC times it was on air and, where the logger saw the stream start, the minute into the recording, so the sponsor can open the video there and see it. The recording is the evidence; the report says where to look. It hides nothing it could not settle, and it carries no price.

What Sponsifer is not

Not a marketplace, not a service, and not an account. It runs on the streamer's own machine and keeps their deals there. Nothing about the streamer leaves it, except one salted hash sent to a public timestamp authority, and only when they choose to seal a file. This site is a set of static pages: every page, the report checker included, is forbidden by its own security policy from making any request.
