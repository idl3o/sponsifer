Check a report or receipt

Choose the files you were sent: the .json and its .sig. They are read by this browser and go nowhere: this page is forbidden, by its own security policy, from making any request.

What a good result proves, and what it does not

  • It proves the file is exactly what the holder of that key signed, and, once you have entered the fingerprint from your agreement, that the holder is the creator you agreed with.
  • It does not prove the placement was on screen. The recording does that: open it at the minutes the report gives. The report is an index into the recording, not proof on its own.
  • It does not prove who is behind a key you never agreed. A good signature by an unknown key is worth nothing, which is why the fingerprint matters.

Check it without this site

The notice that came with the report gives an allowed_signers line and the ssh-keygen -Y verify command. Check that the line's key has the fingerprint in your agreement (ssh-keygen -lf on a file holding the key prints it), then run the command. Reports are signed in the namespace sponsifer-delivery, licence receipts in sponsifer-receipt.
